# Mandarin Songwriting Quality Guide

Use this guide after the creative card and before expanding a Hook.

## Hook quality gate

Do not expand a Hook that fails a material check. A viable Hook has one memorable vocal landing point; when a title exists, test whether the title is the strongest landing point. Use contrast such as a short Hook plus an answering line or repetition plus variation. Keep concrete language, useful rhyme positions, and natural Chinese phrasing; do not sacrifice natural Chinese phrasing for rhyme.

## Lyric quality gate

Before showing a draft, reject a lyric that repeats the theme, piles up abstract emotion words, gives every line equal weight, or changes confirmed choices. In character writing, do not turn a character into a trait list: show actions, scenes, or choices. Repair hard-to-sing clusters and unnatural spoken stress before expansion.

State only one or two material quality risks and the corresponding adjustment. Do not expose a line-by-line technical checklist.
